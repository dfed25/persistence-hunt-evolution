# Persistence Hunt Evolution

An interactive evolutionary simulation of early human persistence hunting.

Simulated hunters chase kudu in the midday heat. Hunters who last longer or catch a kudu have more children, and their traits (hair, sweating, leg length, build, muscle type) evolve over 60 generations.

**Open it:** https://dfed25.github.io/persistence-hunt-evolution/

Made for the UCC AY2008 poster on the endurance running hypothesis (Bramble and Lieberman, 2004). Body heat, sweating, fur and panting use published physiology; see the references on the page.
